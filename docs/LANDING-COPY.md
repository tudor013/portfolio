## About — a letter **[confirmă]**
Before university I competed in robotics: three national awards and a top-half placing
internationally. I liked that a robot is a handful of feedback loops, and that the hard part is how
they behave together.

That is close to what economic cybernetics studies, which is why I chose it. It is also why most of
what I build is a system with moving parts: a market of agents, an airline's bookings, a shop that
has to take an order and a payment and lose neither.

In 2025 I co-founded Aruna Coffee. I designed the brand, drew the shop before it existed, and built
it with an AI builder working from those drawings, with Supabase behind it. The business now sells
over 100 kg of coffee a month through it.

This summer I worked at Soft Tehnica alongside senior developers, on AI work in Python. Since July I
have run the UI/UX division at SiSC, the Cybernetics students' union, where I set up how the division
works and started rebuilding its sites.
