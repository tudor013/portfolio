## The hackathon

We built the platform at ITFest, in a hackathon run together with Innovation
Labs. There were three of us and about one day. We chose the brief for solar
and battery owners and the companies that maintain their installations: their
data is spread across inverters, meters and batteries, and nobody reads it
until something has already gone wrong.

## Research before code

We worked with AI to write the code, but the first part of the day was
research: who uses this data, what they already have, and what they are
missing. That research decided the features and the direction of the
platform, before anything was built.

## What I learned

How an application goes from an idea to a finished product, and what it is
like to build one in a team under a deadline. I also learned a lot about the
statistical models used in the energy industry and how they are applied in
practice. Much of that came from talking with the hackathon's mentors, who
have long experience in energy and in data science.
