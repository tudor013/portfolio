# UX profile — portfolio

Read by the UX and design skills before any change to layout, colour or type.
This file is the contract; `src/styles/tokens.css` is its implementation.

## Direction

**Blueprint, set as a quiet instrument** (taste-skill draw, 27.10.2026, seed
1940305872; the sheet is `.taste-skill/direction.json`). Cool paper, one
near-black ink, blueprint blue. Few things on each screen, and the big things
big: the name, the schema, the market.

The idea the whole landing page carries: *Tudor's own models and drawings
become scenes that run as you scroll.* His airline schema assembles on a
blueprint field, his NetLogo market runs forward in time, his Spline stills for
Aruna pass one into the next.

Removed at Tudor's request (27.10.2026): the liquid glass and the blue light
that followed the scroll. Gradients are not banned outright by him, but none is
used: nothing on the page needs one.

## Theme

Light only. There is no dark variant and no theme toggle. The Software chapter
turns the whole field blueprint blue; that is a chapter colour, not a theme.

## Contrast target

WCAG 2.1 **AA** (4.5:1 text, 3:1 control boundaries), measured with the formula,
never estimated. Every pair is written beside its token in `tokens.css`. The
weakest text pair is soft white on a schema table, 4.70:1.

## Colour

One ground (`--ground`), one ink (`--ink`, soft `--muted` for secondary notes
only), one accent (`--accent`, blueprint blue) used on the one primary action of
a screen. Surfaces are separated by a step in tone (`--surface`), never by a
line or a shadow. The blueprint field (`--field`) belongs to the Software
chapter alone; the hero uses only a faint accent tint for its drafting sheet.

Exception, deliberate: the ID badge is a physical object hanging in front of the
page. It is the one white thing and the one thing with a shadow.

## Type

Literata, one family. 400 for text, 500 for headings, 600 for emphasis, 400
italic for the signature under the letter. Five sizes: `--step-small`,
`--step-body`, `--step-lead`, `--step-head`, `--step-display`. Nothing below 13px;
running text at 17px or more in the full ink. Tabular figures (`.figures`) where
numbers line up, never monospace.

## Banned on the landing page

- Glass (`backdrop-filter`), floating pill navigation.
- Eyebrow labels, tiny letter-spaced capitals, 01/02/03 numbering.
- Hairline dividers and decorative grid lines on ordinary content sections.
  The landing hero is an intentional exception: a faint drafting grid and one
  original elevation drawing establish the blueprint direction before the
  schema scene. Lines elsewhere still belong to real tables, controls and
  diagrams (marked `data-ui`).
- Shadows on resting elements (the badge excepted, above).
- Monospace outside real code.
- Identical card rows; every section has its own composition.
- The default SaaS blue (`#2563EB`): 4.73:1 on this paper.

## Motion

Weighted after design-motion-principles for a creative portfolio: Jakub Krehel
first (entrances with opacity, a short rise and a little blur), Jhey Tompkins
second (scroll-linked scenes), Emil Kowalski for everything under the hand
(140–240ms, ease-out, no motion on high-frequency actions).

- Only `transform`, `opacity`, `filter` and an SVG stroke offset are animated.
- One reader of the scroll for the whole page (`src/scripts/scenes.ts`).
- Entrances: heading, then text, then picture, 80ms apart, once, never hiding
  content before the script has started.
- Reduced motion: every scene shows its finished composition; the badge stops
  swaying but still answers a drag; the market draws its full run at once.
- A top-right motion control switches between full and reduced modes. The first
  visit follows the operating-system preference; an explicit choice is stored
  locally and takes effect before the next page paint.

## Viewports

Desktop first (Tudor's decision), never broken elsewhere: checked at 1440×900,
1366×768, 768×1024, 390×844 and 360×740 with zero sideways scroll. Touch targets
are 44px or more; inline links inside running text are exempt (WCAG 2.5.8).
